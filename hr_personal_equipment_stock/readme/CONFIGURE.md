By default, the stock moves of the accepted requests are created by the
procurement rules, so the requested products need a route that brings
them to the location of the request.

Alternatively, go to *Employees \> Configuration \> Settings*, section
*Personal Equipment Delivery*, and set the *Personal Equipment Operation
Type*. The accepted requests are then delivered with a transfer of that operation type, from
its default source location to the location of the request, without
procurement rules. When *Validate Personal Equipment Transfers* is
checked, these transfers are also validated as soon as all their
products are reserved; the transfers that cannot be reserved, or that
contain products tracked by lot or serial number, are left to be
validated by hand.
